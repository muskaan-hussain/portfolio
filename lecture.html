I love the front of the room.

Architecture  ·  Painting  ·  Interiors  ·  Education

Gratitude and growth are the keys. Public speaking, teaching, giving presentations — this is the part of my work I have always loved, and the one I believe matters most for my future. In April 2025 I returned to my alma mater, Vaishnavi School of Architecture and Planning in Hyderabad, to speak with the final-year students about stepping into the professional world.

It was a privilege to connect with these bright final-year students — their enthusiasm and insightful questions were truly energizing. It further fuels my passion for guiding aspiring talent in the field, and contributing to their growth as they step into the world of architecture.

Teaching is how I give back what every mentor on my journey gave to me.
