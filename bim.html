Architecture of Assembly — The Celestial Trio

Architecture isn't just a structure — it's a symbiotic relationship with the earth. The Celestial Trio reimagines the urban high-rise as a living system: three twisted towers whose aerodynamic form cuts energy loss, wrapped in vertical gardens, crowned by a solar-powered rooftop sanctuary with rainwater collection, and lit through a sun-enclosed atrial collector that powers the building.
