Statement of work and passion

Technology in construction is the area I am most passionate about. My focus is on building tools and automations that make construction management and BIM workflows faster, more consistent, and less repetitive. I have already begun doing this through my professional work and research across projects in the US, UK, and India, and it is the direction I want to continue developing.

Through my experience and conversations with construction teams, I have seen companies use powerful software without fully using the automation capabilities already available in Excel, Revit, BIM platforms, APIs, and scripting environments. Many repetitive tasks can be reduced through relatively small technical changes, and those changes add up when they are repeated across teams, projects, and months of work.

Having worked both on site and on the technology side, I want to bridge the two, so what is built in the model and what happens in the field stay connected.

This is one reason I keep learning new software and programming tools. Coming from an artistic background, I enjoy creating and building new tools, and this has become another form of design for me.

I use AI primarily to learn, test ideas, and accelerate development. The finished solution does not necessarily need AI to function. AI can help me understand logic, test an approach, or develop a VBA command, Python script, Dynamo workflow, or Revit API tool, while the final automation can run independently as code. This also helps maintain privacy because useful tools can operate without sending sensitive project or company information to external AI systems.

Industry researchWho is already doing thisStudying companies using plugins, automation, AI and internal tools in construction, and speaking with the people who use them. Privacy concerns often limit how deeply companies adopt them; I want to help bridge that gap.

What interests me most is developing this work at a scale where small improvements can have a much larger effect. A workflow that saves time for one project engineer becomes far more valuable when it is refined and used across teams and projects. I want to grow as a construction manager while continuing to develop in BIM and VDC, because the value I bring sits between the site and the technology, and I am open to roles on either side of that line at any company doing this kind of work.
